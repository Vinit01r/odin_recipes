Tiramisu

Description

Tiramisu is a famous Italian dessert made with layers of coffee-soaked ladyfinger biscuits, creamy mascarpone cheese, eggs, sugar and a dusting of cocoa powder on top.

It has a soft, creamy texture and a rich coffee flavor. The name tiramisu means “pick me up” in Italian, referring to the energizing effect of coffee in the dessert.

Ingredients

  • Ladyfinger biscuits
  • Mascarpone
  • Eggs
  • Sugar
  • Strong coffee or espresso
  • Cocoa powder
  • Vanilla extract
  • Coffee liquer such as Marsala, rum or Kahlua

Steps

  1. Prepare coffee :- Brew strong coffee or espresso and let it cool.
  2. Make cream mixture :- Beat egg yolks with sugar, then mix with mascarpone cheese to make a smooth cream.
  3. Dip biscuits :- Quickly dip ladyfinger biscuits in a dish.
  4. First layer :- Arrange the soaked biscuits in a dish.
  5. Add cream layer :- Spread mascarpone cream over the biscuits.
  6. Repeat layers :- Add another layer of coffee-soaked biscuits and cream.
  7. Add cocoa powder :- Dust cocoa powder on top.
  8. Chill :- Refrigrate for 3-4 hours before serving.
