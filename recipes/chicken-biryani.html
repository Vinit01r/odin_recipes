Chicken Biryani

Description

Chicken Biryani is a popular South Asian rice dish made with basmati rice, chicken, spices and herbs. The chicken is cooked with aromatic spices and layered with rice, creating a flavorful and fragrant meal loved across India and many parts of the world.

Ingredients

  • Chicken pieces
  • Basmati rice
  • Onions
  • Garlic and ginger paste
  • Yogurt
  • Biryani spices
  • Red chili powder and turmeric
  • Garam masala
  • Fresh coriander and mint leaves
  • Cooking oil or ghee
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Marinate chicken :- Mix chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, salt and spices.
  2. Cook rice :- Boil basmati rice unit it is partially cooked.
  3. Fry onions :- Fry sliced onions until golden brown.
  4. Cook chicken :- Cook the marinated chicken with spices and onions.
  5. Layer rice and chicken :- Place a layer of rice over the chicken.
  6. Add herbs :- Sprinkle mint, coriander and fried onions on top.
  7. Cook on low heat :- Cover and cook on low heat for 15-20 minutes.
  8. Serve hot :- Mix gently and serve hot with raita or salad.
