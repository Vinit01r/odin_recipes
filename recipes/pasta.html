Pasta

Description

Pasta is a popular Italian food made from wheat flour and water. It is shaped into different forms like spaghetti, penne, and macaroni, then boiled and served with sauces such as tomato, cream or pesto. Pasta is known for being simple, versatile and widely enjoyed around the world.

Ingredients

  • Wheat flour
  • Eggs
  • Water
  • Salt
  • Olive oil

Steps

  1. Mix ingredients :- Combine flour, eggs. salt and a little olive oil.
  2. Make dough :- Knead the mixture until it becomes smooth and elastic.
  3. Rest the dought :- Let the dough rest for about 20-30 minutes.
  4. Roll the dough :- Roll it thin using a rolling pin or pasta machine.
  5. Cut shapes :- Cut the dough into pasta shapes like noodles or strips.
  6. Boil pasta :- Cook the pasta in boiling salted water for a few minutes.
  7. Add sauce :- Drain the pasta and mix it with your favourite sauce.
